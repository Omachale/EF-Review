How answers are marked. In typed gap-fills, capitals, straight or curly apostrophes, extra spaces and final punctuation are ignored. Short and full forms count as the same (won't = will not, I'll = I will, doesn't = does not). A student who repeats the word next to the gap (e.g. types "I'll close" after "I") is still marked correct. A missing apostrophe (dont, wont) is accepted with a reminder. Only the answers listed below are accepted. Where first letters are given (vocabulary), students may type the whole word or just the missing letters.
